- HERON (cameo, next-film tease): tall lilac-gray heron with a tiny lantern. Silent.
- NARRATOR: unseen, calm, warm, slightly amused adult storyteller. 145 wpm, 120 on emotional lines, [beat] pauses. Never an expert.
